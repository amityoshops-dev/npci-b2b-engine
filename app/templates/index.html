2 Switch Coordination & CBS Debit[cite: 1]

NPCI switch terminates TLS, decrypts payload via HSMs, and mints an immutable 12-digit Julian Reference Retrieval Number[cite: 1]. It transforms the instruction into an ISO 8583 MTI 0200 debit call sent to the Remitter Core Banking System[cite: 1].

3 Beneficiary Credit & Net Settlement[cite: 1]

Once debited, the switch fires a parallel pacs.008 credit instruction to the payee's bank[cite: 1]. Both legs return 200 OK, the client receives instant confirmation, and the record queues for Circular 222 multilateral net posting at RBI e-Kuber[cite: 1].

PHASE 03

Systemic Architectural Gaps & Edge Vulnerabilities

High-impact failure modes, Deemed Debit lag, and intraday collateral stress.

Gap 01 • High Impact Asymmetric State
Deemed Debits & Reversal Lag

Remitter CBS confirms debit (MTI 0210), but Beneficiary CBS times out on credit instruction. Under Circular 222, dispute auto-reversals are funneled through DC1 (16:00) and DC2 (24:00) dispute batches, causing significant TAT delays for consumer fund restorations.

Mitigation: Move from fixed dispute batches toward adjacent-batch (sub-90 min) auto-reversal pipelines.
Gap 02 • Systemic Risk Collateral Stress
Circular 222 Intraday Liquidity Shocks

In peak cycles (C1: 08:00–10:00 and C8: 19:00–20:30), asymmetric outflows can cause sponsor banks to breach their Net Debit Caps (NDC). If a bank lacks adequate reserve funds at e-Kuber cut-off, the zero-sum settlement batch halts, creating gridlock risk across member institutions.

Mitigation: Continuous Real-Time Liquidity Monitoring (CRTLM) with early-warning collateral triggers at 80% NDC.
Gap 03 • Edge Vectors Protocol Divergence
Peripheral Rail Vulnerabilities

AePS: Silicone spoofing on legacy POS terminals lacking signed liveness tokens. FASTag: Local edge blacklist cache lag allows negative-balance barrier lift. NIPL: Time-zone slippage and FX volatility between foreign central switches and domestic IST clearing batches.

Mitigation: Signed biometric hardware tokens, continuous edge WebSocket sync, and automated Nostro buffers.
PHASE 04

Consolidated PRD Specification & Platform Execution

Four platform-level initiatives, quantitative success metrics, and a 12-month rollout roadmap.

1 Adaptive Switch Circuit Breakers

Deploys continuous TPS health probes at the Layer-7 core. If a bank CBS experiences latency >800 ms or failure rates >5% over 1,000 requests, the switch isolates that adapter to DEGRADED, sheds load, and signals client apps before timeout.

2 Continuous Liquidity Monitoring (CRTLM)

API-first telemetry bridge between participating bank treasuries, NPCI netting accumulators, and RBI e-Kuber. Dynamic early-warning triggers at 80% and 90% Net Debit Cap (NDC) prevent zero-sum failure at cycle cut-off.

3 Next-Gen Event-Driven Dispute Protocol

Decouples auto-reversals from static DC1/DC2 clearing batches. When an asymmetric drop is detected, an automated pacs.004 payment return enters the immediately succeeding clearing cycle, slashing consumer refund TAT from up to 16 hours to <90 minutes.
